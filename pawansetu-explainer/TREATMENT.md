# PawanSetu — "Shop Local, Online" (customer explainer)

Mid-century Cartoon · ~46 s · 1080×1920 vertical · English VO (en-IN female, calm classroom voice) · captions burned in + `.srt`.
The only product claim (confirmed by the owner): **you can shop your local stores online, in minutes.** No delivery times, prices or features beyond that. The app screens are stand-ins drawn in the style.

## 1. Three candidate structures
- **A. Do / don't split** — walking to the shop vs ordering online, rule by rule. Contrast, but it argues against walking to the very shops we promote.
- **B. Onboarding card** — three numbered steps on a phone in a round call-out: open, pick a shop near you, add and order. Teaches the one thing the viewer must do; the "in minutes" claim is shown by how short it is.
- **C. Numbered benefits list** — stamps of claims. Needs more claims than we can honestly make.

**Chosen: B**, framed by **the problem in one gesture** (a held presenter checks her watch: no time to walk to the shop around the corner) and ending on **a checklist ticking itself**, then a dead-still CTA.

## 2. Logline and arc
A busy neighbour learns she can buy from her favourite corner shop in three taps.
Setup (0–10 s): the shop is close, time is short, "here's how". Steps (11–28.5 s): open · pick a shop near you · add and order (silence, then the order click). Ending (28.5–40 s): the shopping list ticks itself; PawanSetu name held still.

## 3. Benchmark
1950s classroom films — learn: one sentence per picture, numbered cards, direct address. Appliance manuals — learn: medallions, curved arrows, round call-outs. Don't take: any character, drawing, lettering or music.

## 4. Colour
Paper ground `#F3E9D2`, ink `#2B2320`. **Product (PawanSetu) = saffron `#E58A3A`, kept all film.** Presenter accent = plum `#7A4E7E` (dress, stars). Step grounds rotate: coral `#E07A5F` (opening), mustard `#E3B23C` (title), powder blue `#8FB8C9` (step 1), olive `#A3AD62` (step 2), turquoise `#4FA9A3` (step 3), coral (payoff), cream (CTA).

## 6. Cue map
Cool-jazz combo at 120 BPM: vibraphone comping (one new vibraphone chord per step), pizzicato walking bass, brushes, flute melody, finger snaps on the title. Each step lands its tap on a downbeat. Silence (everything, ambience included) 26.0–26.5; the order click is the first sound after it. Vibraphone glissando on the stamp; the three step chords return one per tick on the checklist; ring-out under the CTA.
