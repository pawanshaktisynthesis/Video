# "One Day at a Local Shop" — nine styles, one story

~64 s · 1080×1920 vertical · 24 fps · English VO (en-IN female) · captions + `.srt` · ends on PawanSetu.
A showcase of the Lemo-Opuscar library: one shop's day from dawn to night, each hour told in a different style, one style per category. Edit & Documentary and Evidence Films are left out by choice: they need real footage and real figures (owner's decision).

## Structure (chosen: the clock)
Candidates: (A) a relay of one object through nine styles; (B) nine customers, one per style; **(C) the clock: one day, hour by hour — chosen**, because the time of day gives each style a natural reason (dawn = watercolour, a silent-film morning, a pixel-game after-school quest, paper-cut night lights) and the arc (open → busy → quiet) needs no explaining.
