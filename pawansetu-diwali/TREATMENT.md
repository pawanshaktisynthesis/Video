# PawanSetu — "One Small Light" (Happy Diwali)

Paper-cut Lightbox · ~46 s · 1080×1920 vertical · 30 fps · English VO (en-IN female, storyteller) · burned-in subtitles + `.srt`.
Built in Canvas 2D (no GPU in this sandbox): each layer is a cut silhouette drawn per frame with a warm top rim, pulp grain, backlight translucency, a soft cast shadow on the layer behind, depth blur and bloom on the lights.

## 1. Three candidate structures

**A. One window, one night (locked lamp).** A single house front; only the light changes as the night goes from dark to full Diwali. Beautiful but static, and the shops — the point of PawanSetu — never appear.

**B. A relay of gifts.** A delivery scooter travels one long street: it collects mithai from the halwai, diyas from the potter and marigolds from the flower seller, then brings them to one home. Clear PawanSetu story (local shops → your door), natural lateral travel with deep parallax.

**C. Darkness and one hole.** Open on black and one diya flame, then the whole street lights up; end with fireworks and the greeting.

**Chosen: C as the frame, B as the body.** Open on one flame in a home window (darkness and one hole), the lamp turns on and the street wakes up, a near-plane curtain wipes to the first shop, the scooter relays gifts from three shops back to *that same home* (bookend), the box opens and the home's diyas light one by one, then the camera tilts up into the sky, near-silence, a firework, and the greeting writes itself in paper. Last: rack focus to the front row of diyas until the greeting melts into warm light.

## 2. Logline and arc

One small light in a window becomes a whole street glowing, because every gift for the night came from the shops next door.
- **Setup (0–9 s):** darkness, a match, one diya; the lamp turns on, the street wakes.
- **Turn (9.75–30 s):** the PawanSetu scooter collects from three local shops and rides home.
- **Ending (30–46 s):** the box opens, the house lights up, sky, silence, firework, "शुभ दीपावली · Happy Diwali · from PawanSetu", focus melts to the diyas in front.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Lotte Reiniger's silhouette films** — learn: jointed silhouettes read instantly, rotation-only gestures. Don't take: characters or scenes.
- **Commercial paper-cut shadow-box lamps** — learn: value ladder for depth, warm backlight, layer shadows. Don't take: any specific lamp design.

## 4. Ladder and accent

New-moon night (Diwali falls on Amavasya: no moon, only stars and lights), warm violet ladder:
sky `#24163f` (horizon glow `#7a4a7e`) → far skyline `#6b5aa6` → mid houses `#45337a` → story street `#2a1d4f` → scooter `#1d1438` → front `#0e0a1c`.
Light: amber `#ffb862` / gold `#ffd890`. **Accent: PawanSetu saffron `#F08A24`** — only the delivery boxes and the seal.

## 7. Cue map

80 BPM, raga-Yaman colour on D (D E F# G# A B C#). Tanpura drone (D–A) throughout except the silences.
- 1–6: drone + a few slow zither notes (koto model, santoor-like) — the "one light" motif D–F#–A.
- 6.0: temple bell + harp rising arpeggio with the lamp; music box sparkles on window lights.
- 9.75–30: zither melody over harp ostinato; one soft hand-drum stroke per bar downbeat; music box chime on every box landing (12.75, 16.5, 21.0).
- 30–34.5: harp arpeggios, one music-box note per diya lighting.
- 35.25–36.0: near-silence (room tone only). 36.0: burst + full chord swell; motif resolves 39; ring-out to 46.

## 9. Type and subtitles

Cormorant Garamond (OFL) for subtitles and the Latin paper title; Tiro Devanagari Hindi (OFL) for "शुभ दीपावली". Paper words grow from their left edge on the spoken word. Subtitles: warm off-white serif ~42 px with a soft shadow on the dark front band, 64 px from the bottom. The final greeting is paper, not subtitled.
