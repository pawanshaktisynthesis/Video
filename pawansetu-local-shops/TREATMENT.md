# PawanSetu — "Two Streets Away"

Isometric Infographic · 40 s · 1080×1920 (vertical, for Reels / Shorts / WhatsApp Status) · 24 fps · English VO (en-IN female) · burned-in subtitles + `.srt`.

All numbers on screen are illustrative and marked so. PawanSetu wordmark is a stand-in drawn in code (no supplied logo).

## 1. Three candidate structures

**A. One order, tracked.** A single order travels phone → shop → door, ringed "you are here" the whole way. Clear, but on its own it is a delivery-app ad; nothing says *why local*.

**B. Two boards compared.** Board 1: the street today, orders flying off to a distant warehouse. Board 2: the same street on PawanSetu. Strong contrast, but the cut between boards breaks the one-world feeling and spends 10 s on the problem.

**C. The route that comes home.** Open on a home two streets from a shop. Its order line shoots *away*, the camera follows it out to a giant grey warehouse far off-board (the problem in one move). Silence. Then a saffron line — a *setu*, a bridge — draws back from the home to the local shop. The order is tracked through the shop and back to the door, then orders multiply until the whole street is saffron, and a final pull-out leaves a finished infographic.

**Chosen: C.** It contains A's clarity and B's contrast inside one continuous world, and the bridge is the brand's own name (Setu = bridge), so the native move *is* the message. Opening image: a phone in a window, not a logo. Ending: the whole neighbourhood as a legend, not a slogan card.

## 2. Logline and arc

A neighbourhood order that used to travel 30 km to a warehouse comes home to the shop two streets away — and then the whole street follows.

- **Setup (0–10 s):** customers are close; their orders go far. The local shop sits grey, shutter half down.
- **Turn (10–12.5 s):** the saffron bridge draws from the home to the shop; the shop takes the reserved hue, shutter rolls up.
- **Ending (25–40 s):** orders accelerate, every shop on the street turns saffron, pull-out to the full legend.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Stripe / Google Maps isometric product explainers.** Learn: one object tracked through a system, labels that arrive just before the thing they name, very few words per screen. Don't take: their icons, palettes, layouts, type.
- **Kurzgesagt scale cuts.** Learn: a fast log zoom to make a distance felt (2 streets vs 30 km). Don't take: characters, colours, music.

## 7. Cue map

- **Bars 0–2 (0–7.5):** kalimba motif (the "home" motif) + shaker; steel line gets a low synth swoosh, music thins as it goes far (low-pass).
- **Bars 3–3.5:** music drops to a single held bass note, then out at 8.8 → near-silence (wind bed only).
- **Bar 4 downbeat (10.0):** glockenspiel chime (first sound after silence) + marimba enters with the saffron "PawanSetu" motif; bridge blocks land on eighths.
- **Bars 5–9 (12.5–25):** marimba + kalimba + light cajon groove, phone ping on 13.0, cutaway whoosh on 17.0, bag thump, rider bell.
- **Bars 10–12 (25–32.5):** add woodblock; order pops on quarters (bar 10), eighths (bar 11), waves (bar 12); one pitched plink per order.
- **Bars 13–15 (32.5–40):** pull-back, one marimba note per labelled station, resolve on the motif at 37.5, ring out.
